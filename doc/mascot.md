
# Meet Bobo

Bo's mascot is the blue-footed booby [^1] **Bobo**. 

Bobo is Spanish for "fool" (as in *pájaro bobo*, "foolish bird") and the origin of the English word "booby". Big goofy blue feet, a wide-eyed stare, and built-in comedy from the name alone.


[^1]: Blue-footed booby (*Sula nebouxii*), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-footed_booby
